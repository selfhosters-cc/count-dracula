# Last year's numbers

Type: task (HITL)
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

What were last year's half-hour arrival counts, and when did the night start and end? The ghost line needs these numbers written down in a usable form.

Checklist for the human:
- [ ] Each half-hour bucket and its kid count (e.g. `17:30–18:00: 6`)
- [ ] First and last bucket (when you started and stopped answering the door)
- [ ] Anything unusual about last year that might skew the comparison (weather, ran out of candy, closed early)

Record the result as a small data file linked from the answer (e.g. `.scratch/halloween-tracker/data/2025-counts.md`).

## Answer

Recorded in [data/2025-counts.md](../data/2025-counts.md). The 2025 counts per half-hour were: 17:42–18:00: 14, 18:00–18:30: 72, 18:30–19:00: 107, 19:00–19:30: 119, 19:30–20:00: 34. **Total 346.** The drop-off after 19:30 was natural (kids stopped coming, not a candy shortage), so 346 is a fair target to beat, and the ghost line stays flat at 346 after 20:00. The peak of about 4 kids/min means bursts of 8–10 kids at once, which drives phone speed and TV burst handling.
