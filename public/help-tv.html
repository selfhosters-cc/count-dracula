📺 Putting Count Dracula on your TV

The TV faces the street and cheers every kid your phones count. Here's the setup that works best.

What works best

A computer (any laptop or mini PC) plugged into the TV by HDMI, running Chrome or Edge full-screen.

Set it up

  1. On the TV's computer, open Chrome or Edge and go to /tv.
  2. The TV shows a short code and a QR. On your phone, open /setup → Pair a TV and type the code, or scan the QR with your phone's camera.
  3. Press F11 (or the browser's full-screen) so the board fills the TV.
  4. Turn off sleep and screen blanking on the computer for the night, and keep it plugged in.
  5. Click once or press any key so the sound starts. A reload needs a click or key press for sound again: the board shows a 🔇 reminder until then.

The TV remembers its pairing, so next time /tv goes straight to your board.

Other devices

Smart-TV browsers, a tablet in landscape or a cast browser tab may work: open the browser, go to /tv, and pair. They're best effort, so test it on your Rehearsal. If the sound or the screen misbehaves, use a laptop.

Test it

Your household starts with a 🎭 Rehearsal: count a test kid on your phone and watch the TV pop and hear it. Then Reset the Rehearsal in Setup so the TV starts at 0. "Halloween 2026" goes live by itself on Oct 31 at 4:00 pm.
