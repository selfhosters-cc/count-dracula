How it works

Three things on Halloween night. Nothing to install.

1📺 Put a TV on the porch

Any computer on HDMI with Chrome. Open the TV page, scan its code with your phone, and it's paired.

2📱 Tap a costume at the door

Your phone counts, and so does every helper you hand the join link to. One big tap per kid.

3🎉 The TV cheers every kid

Milestones, golden tickets, a race against last year, and a recap and summary to share after.

Got a code?

A code on a TV's screen, or one a host gave you to help at their door or watch along. Type it here: we'll work out which it is. A link you were sent works pasted in too.
